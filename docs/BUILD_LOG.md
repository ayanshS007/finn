**Part A (S2 review fixes)**
- CLAUDE.md contracts: `period_kind` is Q|H1|H2|9M|FY; `Verdict.actual_fact_id` may be null when abstaining or for `went_quiet`. Also added the free-tier rule, and `Fact.has_exceptional_items`.
- Legacy `results-comparision` figures stay labelled standalone (S2 behaviour, test `test_legacy_is_standalone_not_consolidated` unchanged).
- EBITDA now excludes exceptional items: `ebitda = pbt + exceptional_items_added_back + finance_costs + d_and_a - other_income`. For XBRL (Ind AS) filings the amount comes from `ExceptionalItemsBeforeTax`; its sign is read off the filing (`ProfitBeforeExceptionalItemsAndTax - ProfitBeforeTax` is positive for a loss), so a loss is added back and a gain subtracted whichever way a filer signs the line. A nonzero line that the two profit lines do not confirm leaves EBITDA out for that period (logged, in `filing.skipped`) rather than guessing. The formula string stores the added-back amount (`... + 0.00 + ...` when zero). Every fact of a period carries `has_exceptional_items` (true when nonzero, false when the line is zero, null when the source has no line). Q4/H2 differences take the conservative OR of their inputs. Fallback sources: screener nets exceptional items into Other Income, so its plain formula already excludes them (flag null); the legacy payload has `re_excepn_items_new`, and a nonzero value there means no EBITDA for that period. Banks and NBFCs still get no EBITDA.
